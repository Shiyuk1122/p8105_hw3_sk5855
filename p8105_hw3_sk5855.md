The ny_noaa dataset depicts the weather of each stations from 1981 to
2010. There are 7 variables and 2595176 rows of dataset. “date” is the
date of a weather record. “prcp” is the precipitation on this day.
“snow” and “snwd” describe the snow amount and depth on this certain
day. “tmax” and “tmin” are the max and min temperature of the day,
respectively. Missing data are a substantial concern in this dataset,
particularly for temperature: approximately 43.71% of observations are
missing for each of tmax and tmin. Missingness is also present in snwd
(22.80%), snow (14.69%), and prcp (5.62%), while id and date have no
missing values. These missing values reduce the available data for
analysis and may introduce bias if missingness varies systematically
across stations or time periods
